ABOUT LIFELINK

A Better Way to Manage Blood Donation

LifeLink is a Blood Donation Management System that helps blood donation organizations manage donor information, assess donor eligibility, monitor donation status, and organize donation records in one centralized platform.

The system also allows donors to check their eligibility, monitor their donation history, and stay updated with their donation status, making the entire donation process faster, easier, and more organized.

Explore System
THE PROBLEM WE SAW

Making Blood Donation Information Easier to Manage

Blood donation information is often handled through manual records and coordination. This can make donor eligibility, donation history, appointments, inventory, and requests harder to monitor and more prone to inconsistent information.

Less unnecessary processing. More organized information. Easier monitoring. A more convenient experience.
WHY WE BUILT LIFELINK

From a Course Requirement to a Practical Idea

LifeLink began as a Software Design course requirement. As future Computer Engineers, we also wanted to explore how technology can respond to real needs in communities—especially in healthcare. This project is our small attempt to turn that idea into something practical.

OUR ACADEMIC JOURNEY

Learning Through a Real-World Project

LifeLink was developed during the first semester of third year in the BS Computer Engineering program at Bulacan State University – Bustos Campus, as part of the Software Design course. It allowed us to apply software development, design, quality assurance, and consultation to a real-world problem.

WHAT LIFELINK DOES

One Platform for Organized Blood Donation Management

LifeLink brings important blood donation activities into one organized system for easier monitoring.

Donor Management

Organizes donor information in one place for accurate monitoring.

Eligibility & Readiness

Supports health assessment and donor eligibility review before donation.

Donation Scheduling

Organizes appointments digitally and reduces unnecessary coordination.

Donation History

Keeps an accessible record of completed donations.

Blood Inventory

Helps administrators monitor blood stock by blood type.

Blood Requests

Provides a more organized way to submit and manage requests.
