LifeLink Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 14, 2026

LifeLink respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our Blood Donation Management System.

1. Information We Collect

LifeLink may collect information such as:

  • First and last name
  • Username
  • Password
  • Phone number
  • Blood type
  • Donor eligibility information
  • Donation history
  • Account and system activity

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected may be used to:

  • Create and manage your LifeLink account

3. Protection of Your Information

LifeLink takes reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

4. Sharing of Information

LifeLink will not intentionally sell your personal information. Information may only be accessed or shared when necessary for authorized system functions, administration, or when required by applicable law.

Users should keep their usernames and passwords private and should immediately report suspected unauthorized access to the system administrator.

Personal and donation information may be stored for as long as necessary to provide LifeLink's services, maintain accurate records, and meet applicable requirements.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Users will be informed of significant changes when appropriate.

8. Your Consent
